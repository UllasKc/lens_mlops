# LensS MLOps: Command Center walkthrough

What the data is, then every number on the Command Center, top to bottom, with how each is calculated. Figures are as deployed on 13 Nov 2026 (data as of 7 Oct 2026, 23:59).

---

## Part 1: The data

### Two kinds of data

| | **Monitoring data (real source)** | **Operations data (synthetic)** |
|---|---|---|
| What | `model_registry.csv`, `predictions.csv`, `business_outcomes.csv`, `metadata.xlsx` | 7 tables generated by `deploy/synthetic/ops_data.py` |
| Covers | Model health, accuracy, alerts, incidents, savings | Serving uptime and SLA, request volume, latency, cost, input-feature drift, retraining, promotions, governance, recorded causes |
| Period | 5-7 Oct 2026 (3 days, one prediction per model per minute) | Daily, 8 Sep - 7 Oct 2026 (30 days); "this month" = 1-7 Oct |
| Label | None needed | Marked **synthetic** in Unity Catalog, on the page and in the Assistant's answers |

**Why synthetic data:** the Command Center design (from a senior ML leader's proposal) needs SLA, cost, feature drift, retraining, governance and causes. None of these exist in the source files, so they are generated for the **same 100 models**.

### How it is generated

- **Repeatable.** A fixed seed (20261007), so every deploy in every workspace produces identical rows. The deploy's `ingest` step generates the data and lands it; no extra files are needed.
- **Consistent with the real data.** The generator reads the real files first:
  - The **4 models that really drift** (MDL_011, MDL_088, MDL_099, MDL_100) are given high input drift (PSI) on 3-4 features.
  - **Accuracy loss is real**: each model's own error change between the first and last day.
  - Latency starts from the real ~171 ms; a few endpoints are pushed over 200 ms by a recorded incident.
- **Value at stake per model** depends on criticality: High $180K-$650K a month, Medium $40K-$180K, Low $6K-$40K. MDL_061 is set to $950K, as the model behind the biggest real incident.
- **To see the rows:** `D:\Honeywell_MLops\LensS_MLOps_Synthetic_Ops_Data.xlsx` (one sheet per table; the same rows the deploy loads).

### The seven synthetic tables

| Table (silver) | Rows | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| `ops_model_endpoint` | 100 | One endpoint per model: 74 real-time (30 GPU, 44 CPU), 26 batch (11 GPU, 15 CPU); value at stake; last trained; retraining cadence (High 30, Medium 45, Low 60 days) |
| `ops_serving_daily` | 3,000 | 100 models x 30 days: requests and forecast, downtime minutes, p95 latency, errors, cost, utilisation |
| `ops_feature_drift` | 600 | 6 input features per model, named by asset type, with PSI at month start and on the latest day |
| `ops_promotion` | 6 | New versions on the way: 2 canary, 4 staging; 4 on track, 1 at risk, 1 blocked |
| `ops_model_governance` | 100 | Model card and bias-audit dates |
| `ops_pipeline_event` | 6 | Recorded events (below) |
| `ops_event_model` | 32 | Which models each event affected |

### The six recorded events (the only source of a "Recorded cause")

| Date | Where | What happened | Linked to |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20 Sep | lims.lab_results | Lab-results ingestion job failing | Retraining backlog (6 of 9 models) |
| 1 Oct | operations log | Seasonal operating-mode switch | The 4 models losing accuracy |
| 1 Oct | model risk office | Quarterly review opened, due 30 Nov | 11 governance gaps |
| 2 Oct | serving platform | Autoscaling minimum set to 0 on GPU endpoints; cold starts | 5 latency breaches |
| 5 Oct | serving platform | Minimum replicas restored on 2 of the 5 | Those 2 recover |
| 5 Oct | historian.sensor_events | Pressure tags switched from bar to psi | The 4 drifting models |

The Assistant may quote these and never invents a cause. Where none is recorded (the stale models), the page says "none recorded".

### The rules (24 thresholds in `gold.business_rules_config`)

The 4 original rules plus 20 new ones, each with its reason:

| Area | Rule |
|---|---|
| Original | Confidence < 0.60 is low; a model is low-confidence when >= 20% of its latest-day predictions are; drifting at average drift >= 0.30; alert tuning above 25% false alarms |
| SLA and latency | Serving SLA 99.9% uptime; latency SLO p95 <= 200 ms (real-time endpoints only) |
| Drift and accuracy | A feature has drifted at PSI > 0.25; a model has data drift with 3 or more; degrading at error +5%; roll back at +10% |
| Retraining and governance | Stale after 90 days since training; overdue at 14 days past cadence; model card older than 180 days is a gap |
| Upside assumptions | Fixing a degrading or breaching model recovers 25% of its value at stake; right-sizing an under-used GPU saves 50% of its cost |
| Health score | Penalties by tier: Breaching SLO -60, Degrading -40, Stale -25, Watch -10 |

## Part 2: The hero block, "1 · Are we on track this month?"

The month is **1-7 October 2026**, because the data ends on 7 October. Every number comes from the serving data (the **Serving daily** sheet), except promotions (the **Promotions** sheet).

### The sentence

> *"Serving uptime is **99.87%** month to date against the **99.90%** target, with **24 days** left, so the month is behind. If the downtime of the last 3 days continues, the month ends near **99.96%**: likely to meet the target."*

In plain words: slightly below target now, but the problem has eased, so the month should finish above target.

### The six figures

**1. SLA met (uptime): 99.87%**
- The share of time the models were available.
- 100 models x 7 days x 1,440 minutes = **1,008,000 model-minutes**; downtime 1-7 Oct = **1,328 minutes**.
- Uptime = 1 - 1,328 / 1,008,000 = **99.87%**. Target 99.90%, so **0.03 points short**.
- Check in Excel: Serving daily, filter `day` to 1-7 Oct, sum `downtime_minutes` = 1,328.

**2. Predictions served: 493.6M (96% of the 512.3M forecast)**
- Sum of `requests` vs sum of `forecast_requests`, 1-7 Oct.
- Demand slightly under plan: context, not a problem.

**3. Month-end outlook: 99.96%, LIKELY**
- Downtime over the last 3 days (5-7 Oct) is about **36 minutes**, roughly **12 a day**.
- Over the 24 days left, that adds about **288 minutes**; the month total is about 1,328 + 288 = **1,616**.
- The whole month has 100 x 1,440 x 31 = **4,464,000 model-minutes**; 1 - 1,616 / 4,464,000 = **99.96%**.
- **Likely** because the remaining budget allows about 131 minutes a day and we run at about 12. The rule: Likely when the recent rate is at most 80% of what the budget allows per day, Possible up to 100%, Unlikely above.
- A projection, not a forecast; labelled as such.

**4. Error budget left: 70% (1,328 of 4,464 minutes used, burn 1.3x)**
- A 99.9% target allows 0.1% downtime: 0.1% x 4,464,000 = **4,464 minutes** for the month (the "budget").
- Used: 1,328 (30%), so **70% left**.
- **Burn rate 1.3x**: 30% of the budget used / 23% of the month gone (7 of 31 days). Above 1x means spending it faster than the month allows.
- **The bar:** the fill is budget used (30%), the white marker is month gone (23%). Fill past the marker means burning too fast, so the bar is amber.

**5. Promotions in flight: 6 (4 on track, 1 at risk, 1 blocked)**
- New model versions on their way to production: 2 in canary, 4 in staging.
- **At risk:** "Canary error 6% above the live version".
- **Blocked:** "Waiting on lab-results data to retrain" (the same lab-feed failure as the retraining backlog).

**6. The latency callout: 4% of real-time models (3 of 74) are missing the 200 ms SLO today; 5 breached it this week**
- Real-time models must answer within 200 ms at p95; batch models have no such rule.
- 74 are real-time; on 7 Oct, 3 were over 200 ms (3 / 74 = **4%**). Over the last 7 days, 5 breached; 2 were fixed on 5 Oct.
- **View** lists those 5 models with their latency and the recorded cause.

### Why the month is behind

| Date | Recorded event | Effect on the hero |
|---|---|---|
| **2 Oct** | Autoscaling minimum set to 0 on GPU endpoints: cold starts | 5 endpoints over 200 ms, plus 60-110 minutes of downtime each a day |
| 2-4 Oct | Outage period | Most of the 1,328 downtime minutes: uptime falls below 99.9% |
| **5 Oct** | Minimum replicas restored on 2 of the 5 | Downtime drops to ~12 minutes a day: outlook "Likely"; 3 endpoints still slow |

**The message:** "A configuration mistake on 2 Oct put us behind the SLA. It's partly fixed; if nothing else breaks we finish the month above 99.9%. Three endpoints still need fixing today."

### The note under the hero

The two periods in use: **month to date** (1-7 Oct) for serving and **model monitoring** (5-7 Oct) for health and accuracy. It also marks the serving, SLA, cost, promotion and governance figures as **synthetic**; health, accuracy, alerts and savings are the real data.

## Part 3: The executive summary, "The month in 30 seconds"

The box under the hero: the whole Command Center as one bottom line plus five labelled rows.

- **No AI writes it.** The page builds each sentence from the same figures as the chapters, so it can never disagree with the cards.
- Each row has a **label**, **one sentence** and **one big number**. Clicking a row jumps to its chapter.
- It rewrites itself when the data changes (for example "Behind" becomes "Meeting").

### Bottom line

> *"Behind the 99.90% serving SLA month to date (99.87%), and likely to recover by month end; today, roll back 1 model and restore capacity on 3 endpoints over the latency SLO, then retrain the 8 degrading models this week."*

| Wording | Comes from |
|---|---|
| Behind / Meeting | Uptime vs target (hero) |
| likely to recover / no margin / unlikely | The outlook: Likely / Possible / Unlikely |
| "roll back 1 model and restore capacity on 3 endpoints" | The TODAY actions (chapter 4) |
| "retrain the 8 degrading models" | The "Models degrading" card (chapter 2) |

### The five rows

**1. WHERE WE STAND, 99.87% ("uptime month to date")**
> *"Uptime 99.87% against the 99.90% target with 24 days left; 493.6M predictions served (96% of forecast); 70% of the error budget left, burning at 1.3x; 4 of 6 promotions on track."*
- The hero in one sentence. The number is amber when uptime is below target, green above.

**2. THE FLEET, 91 ("fleet health score")**
> *"100 models in production with a health score of 91/100: 57 healthy, 26 on watch, 8 degrading, 6 stale and 3 breaching the SLO; 1 high-risk. $0.152 per 1,000 inferences."*
- 91 is the average model score; the five counts are the tiers (they add up to 100); 1 high-risk = a High-criticality model in a bad tier (MDL_079). Green at 90 or more. Summarises chapter 2.

**3. WHAT'S HURTING US, 5 ("critical or high issues")**
> *"5 real-time endpoints breached the 200 ms latency SLO this week (critical); Accuracy is falling on 4 models (concept drift) (high); 4 more issues ranked below."*
- The top two issues from chapter 3 with their severity. The 5 counts the Critical and High operational issues; red because one is Critical. Chapter 3 shows 9 cards because it adds 3 from the real monitoring data.

**4. THIS WEEK, 4 ("models to act on today")**
> *"Today: roll back 1 model and restore capacity on 3 endpoints over the latency SLO. This week: retrain 4 models with data drift, retrain 3 models losing accuracy, clear a retraining backlog of 9 and investigate 11 low-confidence models."*
- The TODAY and THIS WEEK columns of chapter 4 as a sentence. 4 = 1 rollback + 3 endpoints; red because something is due today. THIS MONTH items are left out for brevity.

**5. EXTRA UPSIDE, $383K ("a month (estimate)")**
> *"About $383K a month: $356K by fixing 11 degrading or SLO-breaching models and $26.8K by right-sizing or retiring 12 endpoints; on top of $48.3M already saved across 5 incidents."*
- $356K value recovery: 8 degrading + 3 breaching models, 25% of their value at stake.
- $26.8K cost saving: 8 under-used GPUs (half their cost) + 4 idle stale models (all their cost).
- $48.3M is the real savings already delivered, shown so the upside isn't confused with value earned.

**The point:** reading only this box gives the message. Behind the SLA because of the 2 Oct mistake, likely to recover; today roll back one model and fix three slow endpoints; this week retrain the degrading models; fixing the fleet is worth about $383K a month on top of the $48.3M already saved.

The deploy also writes a plain-text version to `gold.exec_summary`. The page doesn't show it; the tests use it to check the summary matches the figures.

## Part 4: "2 · How healthy is the fleet?"

### The five cards

| Card | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Models in production | **100** | 74 real-time, 26 batch |
| Fleet health score | **91 / 100** | Each model starts at 100 and loses its tier's penalty; average = (57x100 + 26x90 + 6x75 + 8x60 + 3x40) / 100 = 90.9 |
| High-risk models | **1** | High criticality and Breaching SLO, Degrading or Stale (MDL_079) |
| Models degrading | **8** | 4 with data drift + 4 with accuracy falling (no overlap) |
| Cost per 1K inferences | **$0.152** | $75.0K serving cost this month / 493.6M requests x 1,000 (30-day cost $322K) |

**"Show 10 more metrics"** opens the original monitoring cards (real data): 85 healthy, 4 drifting, 12 low-confidence, 1 business-critical model needing attention, 70% of predictions verified against actuals, 79.7% average confidence, 74% alert precision, 26% false alarms, 97 ms average latency (worst p95 173 ms), 5 models with alerts.

### The fleet by tier

Each model sits in the **first** tier that applies:

| Tier | Models | Rule | Monthly cost | Value at stake |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Breaching SLO | **3** | Real-time, p95 > 200 ms today | $9.3K | $650K |
| Degrading | **8** | Data drift, drifting in the real data, or error +5% | $36.9K | $773K |
| Stale | **6** | Trained more than 90 days ago | $5.5K | $469K |
| Watch | **26** | 1-2 drifted features, low confidence, or retraining overdue | $92.5K | $3.4M |
| Healthy | **57** | None of the above | $177K | $9.3M |

The tiers add up to 100 models and $322K. "Accuracy change" is the tier's average change from the first to the last day (Degrading -2.1%).

**Why 57 healthy here but 85 healthy in the monitoring data:** the tiers also count operational problems (latency, staleness, overdue retraining); the 85 counts only drift and confidence.

### Health by business unit and asset type (real data)

- Most models needing attention: Process Solutions **5 of 21**, then Connected Plant 3/23, Refining 3/19, Aerospace 2/19, Building Controls 2/18.
- Error as a share of actual values: Compressor 21.2%, Pump 20.3%, Heat Exchanger 3.8%, Turbine 2.0%, HVAC 1.5%, Reactor 0.5%, Furnace 0.4%. Compressor and pump models predict small probability values, so the same absolute error reads as a larger percentage.

## Points to make when presenting

1. **Every number is computed in SQL, not by AI.** Each card's **View models** list shows exactly the models it counts, with CSV export.
2. **Synthetic figures are always labelled.** The real health, accuracy, alerts and savings never changed (85 healthy, $48.3M saved).
3. **The outlook is a projection, upside is an estimate** on stated rules, and **causes are quoted only when recorded**.
